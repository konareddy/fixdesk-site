How can we help?

Guides for getting started and everyday work with EagleDesk. The searchable knowledge base and support tickets are coming soon.

Getting started

⬇

Install EagleDesk

Download, open, and let the setup guide check the Salesforce CLI and the AI assistant.
🔗

Connect your orgs

Log in through the normal Salesforce page and choose what EagleDesk may do in each org.
📥

Import your first story

Connect Jira, Azure DevOps or Copado and bring a story in with one click.
🗂

Set up workspaces

One per client: move or share orgs, set the promotion path and work tracking.
🚀

Review and deploy

Approve proposed changes, validate, deploy with tests and a backup.
🧪

Tests and evidence

Run tests with screenshots and get the Word evidence document for the story.

Full articles are being written – until then, the in-app help (the ? button) covers every screen.
